## Three things that shape the whole strategy

**1. Paper-I can eliminate you on its own.**
The advertisement sets the qualifying minimum **in each subject**: 55% for UR, 45% for SC/ST/PH. That means **27.5/50 in Paper-I and 66/120 in Paper-II**, independently. A strong technical paper does not rescue a weak General Ability Test. Paper-I gets a non-negotiable daily hour in this plan, and its 20-mark GK/current-affairs block has no equivalent in any CS question bank — it must be built separately.

**2. Theory of Computation and most Engineering Mathematics are out of syllabus.**
Annexure-C lists no automata, formal languages or decidability, and its Engineering Mathematics consists of exactly one section: *Probability and Statistics*. Skipping ToC, discrete mathematics, linear algebra and calculus removes **~860 GATE questions** from your workload. Those chapters are filed under `ZZ_Outside_TPSC_Syllabus_optional/` in both PYQ trees.

**3. About a quarter of Paper-II has zero GATE coverage.**
Sections 4 (Analog & Digital Communication), 10 (Information Systems & Software Engineering), 12 (Web Technologies), 13 (Cyber Security & Emerging Tech) and 14 (Cloud Technology) total roughly **31 marks** and appear in no GATE paper. They are also the *easiest* marks available, being definitional rather than analytical. This is the single biggest edge in the exam, and it is why `02_State_PSC_PYQs/` exists and why Weeks 9–11 are built around those sections.

## Caveats worth knowing

- **Section-wise weightage for Paper-II is not published by TPSC.** Every marks estimate in these documents is my own, derived from the syllabus ordering and comparable exams, and is labelled as an estimate wherever it appears. All rules, dates, cut-offs and eligibility statements come directly from the advertisement.
- **The negative marking ratio is not stated** in Advt. 02/2026 — Annexure-B only says it applies "as per TPSC norms". This workspace assumes **1/3**. Confirm from the admit card or instructions when they are issued.
- **No written exam date had been announced** as of 02 Sep 2026, and the application window (12 May – 05 Jun 2026) has closed. The 12-week schedule assumes an exam on or after early December 2026; §9 of the study plan covers what to cut if it comes sooner.
- **The OWASP Top 10 is periodically revised.** Learn the current edition by name before the exam.
- The ISRO/NIELIT/UGC-NET compilations are community-maintained (GATE Overflow), not official releases, though they are widely used and include worked solutions. The Kerala PSC and GATE papers are official.
