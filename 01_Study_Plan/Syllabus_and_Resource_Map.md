## Explicitly out of scope

Filed under `ZZ_Outside_TPSC_Syllabus_optional/` in both PYQ folders. Do **not** study these for this exam:

| Topic | Why it's out | GATE questions you can skip |
|---|---|---|
| **Theory of Computation** | No section in Annexure-C mentions automata, regular/context-free languages, Turing machines or decidability | ~293 |
| **Linear Algebra** | Engineering Mathematics is limited to Section 1: Probability and Statistics | ~112 |
| **Calculus** | Same | ~69 |
| **Discrete Mathematics** (combinatorics, graph theory, logic, set theory & algebra) | Same — not listed anywhere in the syllabus | ~390 |

That is roughly **860 GATE questions removed from your workload.** The only caveats:
- Basic **counting/probability** overlaps with Section 1 — covered in Week 9.
- **Graph terminology** is needed for Data Structures and Algorithms (Sections 5 and 6) — covered there, no need for the standalone graph-theory chapter.
- **CFG/grammar basics** are needed for parsing in Compiler Design (Section 7) — covered in Week 11.
